What's next
for Horizon.

An independent app, built to keep improving. Here's what I'm planning to work on next.

  • A layout made for iPhone Duo

    Use both displays comfortably, with a layout that adapts as you open and close your iPhone.

  • A dedicated iPad app

    More space for charts, comparisons and planning, with an interface designed around iPad.

  • Your home, in your retirement plan

    Explore mortgage repayments, overpayments and the financial impact of moving home during retirement.

  • Pension planning for lower-income households

    Model retirement income for lower-income households, including Marriage Allowance eligibility and the potential benefit of transferring part of the tax-free Personal Allowance between spouses or civil partners.

  • Pension planning for higher earners

    More detailed modelling of pension contribution limits, the tapered annual allowance and tax relief.

  • A broader view of your assets

    Track assets beyond pensions and savings, including depreciation, changes in value and potential Capital Gains Tax.

  • Planning for expats and overseas property

    Explore how living abroad, cross-border pension income and overseas property could affect retirement plans, including tax and residency implications.

  • More detail before retirement

    Bring earnings, salary sacrifice and the percentage you save into the picture before retirement starts.

  • Model pension contributions

    Explore employee and employer pension contributions, including SIPPs.

  • Plain-English learning

    Educational articles to make UK pensions and tax concepts easier to understand.

  • Spending that fits your retirement

    Set more detailed budgets and adjust individual spending categories at different stages of retirement.

  • Keeping pace with UK rule changes

    Regular updates for relevant changes to UK pension, tax and employer National Insurance rules.

Premium removes adverts and helps fund ongoing development. Development priorities may evolve with your feedback.
