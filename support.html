Premium & subscriptions

How do I cancel?

In the app, open Settings → Manage annual subscription. You can also open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Horizon Premium. Cancel renewal there. Access normally continues until the paid period ends. Apple's cancellation guide.

How do I restore Premium?

Use Settings → Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple account used to subscribe. Check that the subscription is still active. Contact support if access does not return.

Does resetting my plan cancel Premium?

No. Resetting changes the plan stored on your phone. Apple manages the subscription separately.

Can I request a refund?

Apple handles refund requests through Report a Problem.

Your plan and reports

Is my plan uploaded?

The app stores the plan on your iPhone. It is not sent to the developer or added to ad requests or diagnostics. Reports leave the app only when you choose to share them. See the privacy policy.

Are projections guaranteed?

No. They illustrate your entered assumptions. Tax, pension rules, returns and real spending can change. Check official pension statements and seek regulated advice for personal decisions.
